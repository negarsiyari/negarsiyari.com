<CaseStudySection title="What it is">

AI Without Borders is a practical learning initiative for people who are often
left out of new technology conversations: newcomer, refugee, and multilingual
adult learners. The project treats generative AI as something people can learn
through use, reflection, and support, not as a tool reserved for already fluent
or highly technical users.

</CaseStudySection>

<CaseStudySection title="The approach">

The work centers on everyday tasks: writing clearer messages, exploring job
options, preparing for interviews, understanding unfamiliar systems, and asking
better follow-up questions. Instead of teaching AI as a set of abstract rules,
the project teaches learners how to describe context, evaluate answers, revise
prompts, and decide when human support matters more than automation.

</CaseStudySection>

<Quote cite="Project principle">
Access to AI literacy should not depend on already having the language,
confidence, or institutional access that AI can help people build.
</Quote>

<CaseStudySection title="Why it matters">

Generative AI can widen existing gaps when only the most resourced learners get
guided practice. It can also become a bridge when instruction is designed around
real needs, plain language, and learners' own goals. AI Without Borders explores
that second possibility: AI literacy as a form of language, workforce, and civic
access.

</CaseStudySection>
